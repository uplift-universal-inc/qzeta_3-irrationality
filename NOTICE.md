# Rights and provenance

The imported `lean/` package retains its original Apache-2.0 license and citation metadata. The original construction engine and proof sources are retained with their source records; no new license is assigned to those materials or to the manuscript in this preparation step. The owner should choose the manuscript and code licensing before a public release.

The editing workflow used `latex-math-writing` and `math-paper-campaign` from Amélie Loher's `math-paper-skills`, adapted from work by Scott Armstrong and Amélie Loher (CC BY 4.0), repository revision `e2f73a884ae32487d02dfd10ce8619121daf95d6`. Those skills are editorial methodology, not mathematical proof sources. Their files are not bundled here.
